How honest labels work

Tap a claim to see its verification. Downgrade rather than pretend.

"LLMs fabricate 23.4% of citations in academic drafts."

VERIFIEDSource exists (CrossRef DOI match) and the abstract substantively supports the claim.

"Our method outperforms all baselines on every benchmark."

UNVERIFIEDNo source attached — this is an author assertion. Rewrite as hypothesis or add evidence.

"According to 2019 statistics, 40% of enterprises suffered attacks."

STALE:2019Data is 7 years old — find a newer source before submitting.

"This direction could lead to a fundamental breakthrough in materials science."

EXTRAPOLATEDFrontier-track inference, not verified knowledge. Kept separate from empirical claims.

"The dataset contains 1.2M annotated examples (Smith et al.)."

CITEDSource exists, but content not yet verified against the abstract. Downgraded honestly.
